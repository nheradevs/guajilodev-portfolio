About

I started in 2011 in tech support in Venezuela, fixing administrative systems that were already in production. From there I moved into development and never left the problem side: I maintain, fix, and modernize.

I’ve worked with teams and clients in Venezuela, Chile, Argentina, Ecuador, Mexico, and Spain, always remotely. I’ve led small teams since 2017, and I’d rather review code than give speeches.

Today I use models and agents every day. That didn’t make me faster at everything. It made me stricter about what I review. A model writes in two seconds what then has to be read, understood, and tested. That part doesn’t get delegated.

I don’t sell digital transformation, and I won’t tell you AI solves everything. I use the tool that solves the problem, and when something doesn’t, I say so.

Native Spanish. English B1, enough to read technical documentation and work on distributed teams.
